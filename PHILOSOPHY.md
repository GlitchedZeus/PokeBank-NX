# PokeBank NX Philosophy

> **Nintendo-level polish. Homebrew-level freedom. PKHeX-class power. Kid-safe by default.**

PokeBank NX should feel like Nintendo and the Pokémon homebrew community collaborated on the ultimate Pokémon management app: polished and intuitive for normal players, powerful enough for experts, preservation-minded, offline-first, and designed around the features people actually want.

The engine underneath can be extremely technical. The player-facing experience should make that power feel safe, obvious, and enjoyable.

The canonical design standard is:

[`docs/PRODUCT_UI_PHILOSOPHY.md`](docs/PRODUCT_UI_PHILOSOPHY.md)

That document defines the child-safety test, progressive disclosure, generation-aware UI, preview-vs-mutation boundaries, controller/touch behavior, visual destination selection, truthful legality/provenance, dialog sizing, Homebrew-community values, and the design-review checklist for future features.

This is a design aspiration, not a claim of affiliation with or endorsement by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, GAME FREAK, or Creatures.
