# PokeBank NX Legality Engine

Status: **active foundation work**

PokeBank NX already had an informational structural/internal checker. The legality engine extends that work into an exact-game, evidence-driven analyzer for the generations the application currently supports.

## Design rules

- Legality analysis is **read-only**. It never mutates or auto-fixes a Pokémon.
- A clean report means **no problem was found by the checks that ran**. It is not automatically proof that the Pokémon is fully legal.
- Every analysis area tracks coverage. Missing evidence is reported as incomplete coverage rather than silently treated as legal.
- Exact save identity matters. Ruby, Emerald and FireRed share the PK3 entity format but do not share every legal move or encounter.
- Historical mechanics stay historical. Gen I/II DV + Stat Experience rules are not replaced with modern IV/EV assumptions.
- Encounter, move, PID/RNG, event/gift, egg, trade and transfer legality are separate evidence layers.
- Event/gift coverage is tracked separately from retail static encounters: recognizing a Mystery Gift PID pattern does not prove an exact distribution template.
- A recognized Gen IV Mystery Gift anti-shiny PID/IV class raises Event/Gift coverage to **Partial**; it stays incomplete until a specific WC4/PCD/PGT template matches.
- Legality never changes save-write authorization.

## Planned layers

1. **Format / structure** — ids, checksums, field ranges and format invariants.
2. **Internal consistency** — level/EXP, names, gender, ability, forms and stats. Exact DP/Pt/HGSS native form counts and historical two-slot ability tables are enforced before Gen IV encounter evidence is accepted; native Gen IV-origin PK4 ability identity must also match the PID-selected native slot. Pal Park ability-slot edge cases remain conservative rather than forcing the native Gen IV rule.
3. **Exact-game move legality** — compatibility tables are wired now; event/tutor chronology and full tradeback provenance remain to be completed.
4. **Encounter provenance** — Gen I released R/B/Y static/trade templates, Gen II Gold/Silver/Crystal static/gift and fixed in-game trade evidence, and Gen IV wild, static/gift, and fixed in-game trade evidence are imported; fixed Gen IV NPC trades also preserve their source-proven first/second ability slot; Gen IV static evidence also preserves fixed gender/nature/shiny/fateful constraints where the pinned template explicitly supplies them, and rejects the unreleased Korean Shaymin static case; Stadium/external events and Japanese Blue remain incomplete. Gen IV NPC-trade stored language-ID quirks are enforced for the D/P English-to-Japanese bug, Meister Magikarp German/English swap, and Lt. Surge Pikachu English/French swap; localized nickname/OT string matching remains separate incomplete evidence. PokéWalker course-slot data is pinned as positive species/level/gender/move evidence, and evolved descendants now retain provenance through the pinned Gen IV pre-evolution chain; course unlock/language history and remaining per-method restrictions are still incomplete.
5. **PID/RNG correlation** — Gen III handheld Methods 1/2/3/4 plus the truncated R/S/FRLG roamer class, category-aware Gen IV static/gift PID evidence, normal Gen IV Method-1 PID/IV, deterministic PokéWalker PID plus pinned 27-course/162-slot encounter evidence, Cute Charm buffered-PID surfaces, Chain Shiny PID/IV/trainer-ID correlation, Gen IV Mystery Gift anti-shiny ARNG rerolls, and positive Method J/K Grass/Surf correlation for both no-lead and successful Synchronize frames, successful Synchronize correlation for ordinary fishing/Headbutt/Honey Tree and normal Rock Smash activation, plus no-lead Old/Good/Super Rod slot+level+activation correlation, plus HG/SS Headbutt, HG/SS Rock Smash activation (normal and Illuminate), HG/SS fishing activation including the Old Rod Suction Cups/Sticky Hold branch, D/P/Pt Honey Tree level correlation with trainer-ID-specific Munchlax tree provenance, and direct HG/SS Bug Contest/Safari Method K paths are recognized. Bug Contest/Safari direct evidence enforces the minimum-one-31-IV acceptance rule when the current candidate itself contains a 31 IV; the legal exhausted fourth-attempt no-31 reroll history remains incomplete. historical daily Feebas-tile occupancy itself, non-Sweet-Scent Bug Contest deadlock histories, Synchronize Bug Contest/Safari reroll paths and Radar-special frames, plus Cute Charm/Static/Magnet Pull/Pressure/Intimidate lead-frame branches, PokéWalker IV/course-unlock provenance, event/form-specific Chain Shiny encounter reconstruction beyond direct or base-form ancestor Radar-capable wild rows, remaining Gen IV event distribution-history linkage, plus Gen III BA-CD different-OT/template restrictions and recursive CXD shadow-team/shiny-skip history. The pinned 90-row XD shadow identity table and 50-row PCNY machine-gift table now provide positive persistent-field evidence; unreconstructable team/distribution history and other Gen III event-template linkage remain incomplete.
6. **Egg / breeding legality** — native Gen III/IV egg-state structure, exact-generation/game egg moves and generation-filtered pre-evolution retention are checked now. Hatched PK4 egg-origin records are validated against the pinned 235-entry DP/Pt/HGSS hatch-location table; Link Trade eggs may hatch in any valid Gen IV location, while the Korean Seabreak Path / Flower Paradise release restriction is enforced first. The egg-move table is positive evidence only; exact parent-pair feasibility and Masuda PID rerolls remain incomplete. Pinned PokéPark DS Download event eggs, Pokémon Center Japan 5th Anniversary eggs, and Pokémon Box recipient eggs now have positive unhatched PK3 template/RNG evidence; all unhatched EncounterGift3 egg paths enforce the source conversion's Japanese-language invariant. the PCJP family additionally validates the weighted species/moveset/shiny table selected two RNG calls before the BA-CD sequence, while Pokémon Box validates its four fixed event movesets without inventing trainer-ID constraints. Twenty-five PCJP/PCNY/PokéPark Wondercard eggs also have positive unhatched evidence using Japanese language, their fateful/event location, exact moves, source game group, and the documented Method 2 plus Method 1/4 VBlank-compatible PID classes. Post-hatch event-egg reconstruction remains incomplete.
7. **Event / gift legality** — a generated 247-template Gen IV WC4/PCD Pokémon index now provides positive direct-template evidence for invariant fields including exact native event met location; same-species form changes allowed by the pinned Gen IV FormInfo rules retain event provenance, while impossible exact-game forms are rejected before matching. Ranger Manaphy PGT egg/trade provenance is recognized separately. OT text/trash bytes, ribbons, dates, form-specific source evolution, traded-recipient identity after hatch, and remaining distribution restrictions are still incomplete. Form-0 WC4/PCD source templates now retain positive provenance through the pinned Gen IV evolution chain.
8. **Transfer legality** — Gen III -> IV Pal Park marker/state validation, D/P versus Pt/HGSS split-location patterns, and Gen I/II Time Capsule compatibility/origin evidence are active. Split-ball/external-event provenance, exact historical Time Capsule direction where data is ambiguous, and later transfer chains remain incomplete.
9. **Bulk/provenance checks** — duplicate identities, clone lineage and Vault history once the Vault backend exists.

PKHeX is used as a reference implementation for architecture and cross-checking. PokeBank NX does not claim PKHeX parity until equivalent evidence and regression coverage exist.
