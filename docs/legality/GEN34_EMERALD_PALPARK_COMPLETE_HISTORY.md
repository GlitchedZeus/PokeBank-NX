# Emerald → Pal Park → Gen IV complete source-attested move history (TRANSFER-002)

This milestone connects three formerly separate read-only candidate checkers without claiming that a detached PK3 or PK4 reveals a real creature's history.

1. The same externally identified Emerald source must supply a complete four-slot pre-Pal Park learning timeline and the **identical** verified outbound snapshot, as already required by `Gen3EmeraldPalParkJointPath`. No Gen III HM may be carried through the migration boundary.
2. The same immediate Gen IV Pal Park arrival snapshot must be independently attested twice with **matching, nonzero historical-stage tokens**. All source-game, destination-game, species, exact four moves and availability witnesses must agree. An individual move learned *after arrival* (including a Gen IV HM) does not imply it was illegally retained through Pal Park.
3. The full post-arrival four-slot teaching sequence must be independently evidenced with the correct increasing event stages, bounded levels, complete source-method evidence and deterministic forget-slot transitions. The final proposed move snapshot is computed from that history, not an unbounded union of level-up/TM/HM/tutor lists.
4. A separate **source-attested same final moment** must link to an immutable, valid, non-egg, checksum-valid encrypted native PK4 from the claimed game-group save. Match its original four physical move slots and species to the single reconstructed observed snapshot; do not infer game history from an origin/version marker or PID coincidence.
5. The independent same-creature chain, source-game group, two exact stage equalities and final PK4 source continuity must all be evidenced. Missing identity/stage/source/method evidence returns Unknown, while inconsistent *supplied hypothetical histories* are locally contradicted. `KnownConditionalFullPath` is **not provenance authenticity, full legality or general permission to write**.

**Scope:** verified original Emerald GBA histories with Diamond/Pearl/Platinum/HeartGold/SoulSilver NDS Pal Park destination groups. This is explicitly bounded to the original single transfer and observed post-arrival move sequence. No Gen V, other generations' transfer pipeline, event-original alternatives, original egg stages or unverified move teaching claim is silently accepted. The native PK4 is read-only. Production UI/source save/staged editing and umbrella legality PR #103 remain untouched.

**Tests:** a working conditional native encrypted Platinum observation after Emerald source and Pal Park arrival, two successive later move teachings (one HM learned only after transfer), original/encrypted byte preservation, and negatives for missing creature provenance, missing/inconsistent stage pairings, altered arrival snapshot, missing final PK4, mismatched source group, altered encrypted final moves, incomplete method evidence, illegal GBA-carried HM, unavailable original learning, unsupported source/destination, and invalid native observation. These are fabricated paired snapshots to test API invariants, NOT externally authenticated paired retail save histories.

## Multiple original Emerald–Pal Park hypotheses — TRANSFER-002 candidate universe

`Gen34EmeraldPalParkCandidateUniverse` provides bounded evaluation of up to eight independently source-identified alternative full histories against the **same original encrypted observed native PK4 bytes and source group**. Zero or duplicate identifiers, missing sources, a different final Pokémon, unsupported native evidence and capacity exhaustion cannot be used to inflate the number of defeated candidates. One independently attested consistent route remains a useful *conditional* route regardless of how many other routes are unenumerated. A negative `AllAttestedEmeraldRoutesContradicted` result requires **each** independently grounded candidate to be defeated by a known local contradiction with **complete independently covered acquisition methods**, plus an external statement that the Emerald→Pal Park candidate universe is complete. This does not exclude Ruby/Sapphire/FRLG-original routes, gifts or other legitimate origins; no global Invalid claim is ever made.

The host/sanitizer regression uses the same immutable native Platinum record to compare one compatible, one incompatible, and one source-unknown Emerald/Park/Post-Park chronology simultaneously, plus independent negative candidate sets, incomplete universe/method evidence, duplicated IDs, missing original PK4, and borrowing a different native encrypted record. No archive or historical evidence is synthesized by the library itself; all test records are explicitly hypothetical.
