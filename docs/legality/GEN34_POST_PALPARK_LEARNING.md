# TRANSFER-001 — strictly staged Gen IV post-Pal Park move history

Original GBA move slots and immediate Pal Park arrival have a separately source-attested bounded conditional verifier. Real Pokémon may learn **new moves only after the transfer**; treating the original GBA as though it carried postmigration Gen IV methods produces false conclusions. `Gen34PostPalParkLearning` reconstructs the exact observed four-slot move state from the validated transfer arrival plus an **independently provided, complete, ordered** sequence of moves subsequently taught in the same game/individual.

Each teaching step has independently verified source method, current level, increasing stage ordinal, move ID in the Gen IV domain, and exact replacement or first-empty slot. A prior GBA HM cannot migrate across the boundary, but a Gen IV HM (e.g. Cut #15) may be independently taught *after* arrival. An unresolved method or undocumented same-game continuity stays Unknown rather than disqualifying the specimen.

Tests exercise two hypothetical source-attested Gen IV learning operations (Cut HM #15 and Toxic TM #92), matching final order and source, plus missing pre-transfer proofs, guessed method families, duplicated moves, out-of-range Gen V move IDs, nonmonotone stage/levels, wrong final order, unlicensed GBA HM carry, fake new destination game, capacity overflow and source discontinuity. These explicit synthetic stage attestations are not actual independently recovered retail trainers or native paired PK3/PK4 historical proof.

No automatic fixes, edits to source/staged Pokémon, permission changes, global Invalid, or merged MAIN feature changes.
