# L1 E17/E18 original encounter-candidate universe and conservative reporting

The new `Gen14EncounterCandidateUniverse.h` combines results from eight **specific encounter-history** helpers without modifying the central `Legality.cpp` report or another lane's code. Source-family adapters cover Red/Blue/Yellow static/trade, Gold/Silver/Crystal wild and static/gift, Gen III exact Safari and Colosseum/XD shadow, Emerald original Method H, exact original Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald/FRLG roamer capture-stage forward PID+IV history, GBA/GC/Channel/BACD standalone RNG family-only stage, Sinnoh Wild/Honey/Radar, Gen IV original static/gift/roamer, HGSS special wild stages, independently source-linked DPPt/HGSS Method J/K wild and standalone Method J/K nature-reversal stage. Additional original-history contradictions are preserved as Unknown when incompatible witness flags are simultaneously attested (such as both Time Capsule and no-Time-Capsule), or an unrecognized enum route is passed; a caller cannot smuggle such inputs into false certainty. Source grades and pinned reference files are documented in this PR's four other L1 evidence reports (PKHeX `6501f0ab46e8f8ca048539dbaf8cae8cb104e722`; research `#252@fc0225e6...`). It is a coordinator integration API, **not** a finished global solver.

## Interpretation contract

Every candidate carries exact family and a source-stage provenance-attested bit. Local row/PID/ball/method/team negatives remain `CandidateLocalContradiction`. A matching **standalone** Gen III PID/IV family or Gen IV J/K nature-reversal stage is template/stage-only, never a completed capture. The genuine joined Emerald Method H and previously accepted Gen IV source-linked wild status are conditional histories. Missing original title/save, cross-title row, absent tree/lead, bounded RNG search exhaustion or unprovable capture stage are `UnknownOriginalHistory`. Matching an original **template alone** (`Gen I/II static/trade/wild`, `Gen IV Honey/Radar`, `Gen IV gift egg awaiting L2 RNG`) becomes `OriginalTemplateCompatible`. A candidate with a joined stage-specific original RNG proof (`Gen3 Safari`, `CXD shadow`, `HGSS special original Method K`) becomes `ConditionalOriginalHistory`, explicitly not the authenticity of an arbitrary detached specimen.

`summarize()` retains the competing histories and separate counts, prioritizing conditional, template-only, unknown and finally an **attested candidate set's local contradictions only**. It has **no Invalid output** even if every supplied candidate fails: source universe completeness, evolutions/trades, unobserved original RNG and archival special distributions cannot be inferred from a list a caller happened to supply. Missing independently recorded original-source provenance downgrades even mathematically compatible results to Unknown. The caller must carry loss-of-source uncertainty and cannot use this helper as a false-Invalid hard gate.

## Tests/limitations

`test_legality_l1_original_candidate_universe.cpp` includes compile-time exact enum mapping controls (including GameCube SearchLimit, HGSS bounded search Unknown, source-only Radar and gift-egg, foreign game row), conditional-vs-template precedence, competing duplicate physical source-row candidates and a two-candidate all-local-negative case that **still does not** support global Invalid. Tests form the eleventh native L1 suite compiled both O2/-Werror and ASan/UBSan by the lane-specific standalone runner. **These are authored tests, not claimed executed results**.

E17 exhaustive historical candidate enumeration remains fundamentally blocked without full source families, native original saves/parent/trade/trigger witnesses, and original game-specific condition multiset verification (L5 Q04/Q06/Q09). E18 is a source-safe reporting contract with **no production wiring** until coordinator-owned code, full exact combined-head CI and independent false-Invalid audit. No source mutation, MAIN merge, Gen V, Master Vault, shared Makefile/workflow or other lane edits.
