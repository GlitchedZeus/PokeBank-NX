# TRANSFER-001 — Gen III Pal Park immediate move-stage evidence

In retail Diamond/Pearl/Platinum/HeartGold/SoulSilver, Pal Park moves a Gen III Pokémon *forward only*; Pokémon retaining any prohibited Gen III HM on the outgoing snapshot cannot migrate (Bulbapedia Pal Park and HM reference; original exact retail code must still be audited). To validate a specific hypothetical transfer, `Gen34PalParkSnapshotTransition` requires independent testimony/fixtures for the **exact outgoing four slots** and **immediate arrival four slots**, from an attested Gen III cartridge game and valid Gen IV destination. The two snapshots must have preserved move order, IDs ≤354 on GBA, and no prohibited Gen III HM at crossing. Move deletions *before* transfer or new Gen IV learning *after* transfer must be their own stages. Never infer that an unchanged stored Gen IV PK4 tells us the pre-transfer source move list.

Distinct outcomes: unsupported retail combination, unknown/missing stage provenance, malformed hypothetical source data, candidate mismatch, and candidate-consistent four-slot transfer. Every mismatch excludes only the asserted stage, **not** all real-life histories or all sources of the Pokémon. No false global Invalid or source/staged mutation.

Dedicated host and ASan/UBSan tests cover all eight Gen III HM IDs, a retained four-slot non-HM moveset, new Gen IV-only move incorrectly asserted on immediate arrival, altered move order, unknown pre/post stage, unsupported source/destination games, out-of-gen species and malformed slot sequences. Stage data is synthetic and has not been relabeled as a genuine archived Gen III→IV Pokémon pair. Source data and copyright/licensing remain separate.
