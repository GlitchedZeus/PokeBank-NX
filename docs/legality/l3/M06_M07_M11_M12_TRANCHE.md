## Major tranche 2 — ordered retail offers and machine-item provenance (2026-10-12)

The previous isolated L3 replay accepted an asserted complete chronology without independently testing that **every source-attested level-up offer** between its two snapshots was represented. That is an important false-positive risk: correct final moves do not establish that the path supplied all mandatory offer/auto-learning decisions. This follow-up adds bounded *conditional* exact-replay evidence without modifying the main analyzer or rewriting the historical accepted Emerald timeline.

## Major tranche 3 — exact Pal Park transfer joint stage (2026-10-12)

**M11/M12 substantial production addition** in L3-owned include/Legality/Gen14CandidateMovePalParkJointStage.h with independent, source-conditioned native positives/contradictions/Unknown tests at tests/test_legality_l3_palpark_joint_stage.cpp. This is a **read-only hypothetical candidate witness**, not a retail acquisition validator that guesses lost history.

### The previously unjoined evidence

An individually consistent Gen III four-slot timeline and independently consistent Pal Park snapshot must refer to the **same original candidate**, exact source game **at departure**, exact destination game, observed level and **identical predeparture four slots**. The old independent helpers did not prove that join. A final PK4 move compatibility pool likewise does not attest later teaching. The new joint helper composes, rather than modifies or forks, accepted Gen34PalParkSnapshotTransition.h, the L3 Gen14CandidateMoveChronology.h, and L3 Gen14CandidateMoveMachineEvidence.h.

The verifier:

1. Requires attested **same original Pokémon** across chronology and crossing; exact game and species identity; one verified Pal Park action in a source-GBA→destination-NDS bounded path. Its exact-game mapping covers Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed, LeafGreen → Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold, SoulSilver, without collapsing the five origins or five destinations into a generic generation.
2. Finds the **actual last GBA game visited** before the transfer action. For example, a source-verified Ruby→Emerald visit and Emerald→SoulSilver Pal Park is validated against **Emerald** as the departure; a falsely relabeled Ruby departure is not silently accepted.
3. Truncates the same witnessed four-slot chronology **immediately before Pal Park** and independently replays its exact prefix to the cited verified outbound Gen III four slots. Requires the predeparture four slots equal the verified immediate NDS arrival slots; the accepted transfer helper rejects an old GBA HM in the outbound snapshot (M17 safety), and the L3 full replay independently protects the edge.
4. Binds **every original GBA TM/HM Learn action** to exactly one separately attested original-game species-specific **numbered retail item** step. The currently source-pinned production machine eligibility subset is the full 19-item Bulbasaur GBA set; a verified TM06→Hidden Power item mismatch contradicts that candidate path, while unknown species, non-pinned item or missing witness stays Unresolved.
5. Binds **every later Gen IV Learn, Decline, AlreadyKnown or DeleteMove action** to an independently verified method-specific source record at the same exact game, move, level and step index. This deliberately does **not** infer a Gen IV source from broad species compatibility. A valid source-pinned later reacquisition can be witnessed after the Pal Park edge; the same HM before the edge cannot be carried across. A missing original-method record, changed snapshot, irrelevant extra witness or duplicated provenance witness remains Unresolved. A Gen IV Move Deleter stage is distinct from teaching: its independently attested service record retains the chronology's methodSource=Unknown contract, yet allows a real later deletion when source-record and exact stage are independently supplied; no deletion-source packet is inferred from a compatibility table.
6. Runs the **complete** source-to-final chronology again after the boundary join, returning only CandidateConsistent, CandidateContradicted, Unresolved or Unsupported. None of these can be promoted to global Invalid without exhaustive independently proved alternate candidate histories.
