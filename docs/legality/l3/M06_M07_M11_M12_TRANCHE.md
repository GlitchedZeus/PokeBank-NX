## Major tranche 2 — ordered retail offers and machine-item provenance (2026-10-12)

The previous isolated L3 replay accepted an asserted complete chronology without independently testing that **every source-attested level-up offer** between its two snapshots was represented. That is an important false-positive risk: correct final moves do not establish that the path supplied all mandatory offer/auto-learning decisions. This follow-up adds bounded *conditional* exact-replay evidence without modifying the main analyzer or rewriting the historical accepted Emerald timeline.

### M06: complete Emerald source-table reuse, restricted RS/FRLG version facts

- New owned `include/Legality/Gen14CandidateMoveGbaLevelUpTimeline.h` explicitly enumerates all offers between independently attested initial/observed level snapshots. It references the existing accepted `Gen3EmeraldLevelUpEvidence.h` and committed 386 National Dex/3,947-entry Emerald table, not a newly copied third-party dataset. Rows are read in retail source order; two offers at level 15 remain two distinct decisions. Identical slot totals alone cannot hide a missing or re-ordered offer.
- Ruby/Sapphire and FireRed/LeafGreen are **not** mapped to Emerald's table. For these four games the helper contains only independently pinned original Bulbasaur/Charmander full level-up rows (11 and 10 offers respectively). Exact original game identity is retained. All other R/S/FR/LG species return `Unresolved`, even if supplied hypothetical flags claim the path was complete.
- Source-verified Charmander lvl 13 is **Smokescreen (move 108)** in R/S/E but **Metal Claw (232)** in FR/LG; Flame/Move identifiers checked against pinned source constant files. The helper proves conditional source-specific move-offer compatibility, **not** actual acquisition or the original Pokémon's real birthplace.
- Verified initial four-slot snapshot, same exact species and game through the claimed interval, absence of outside move changes, and every offered player decision are independent required fields. Missing any field => `Unresolved`. A wrong concrete offer or impossible slot choice contradicts only the verified candidate route. The helper never issues global `Invalid`.
- In-scope cases: insert when space available, ordered full-slot replacement, decline when full, already known, duplicate same-level offers, exact stage bounds. Cross-game visits/evolution are a separate historical graph, **not** silently accepted in a same-game level-up-only proof.

Exact original sources: [pokeruby@63a8cbf level-up table](https://github.com/pret/pokeruby/blob/63a8cbf0016b351a4e68f7036fa0b77e23d2f2c1/src/data/pokemon/level_up_learnsets.h), [pokeemerald@5eff786 level-up table](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/blob/5eff78649e7170a877b961ef0b3da13b81a16038/src/data/pokemon/level_up_learnsets.h), [pokefirered@c75f352 level-up table](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/c75f352304d529f6ba92d4f74b9cf8b5c3810788/src/data/pokemon/level_up_learnsets.h).
