## Hard safety and completeness limits

A learned move compatible with a source species is not proof that the same Pokémon could retain the **full four move set** through all evolution, event, egg, trade, tutor, overwriting and migration stages. The all-species original **source method tables** are now populated, but original teacher/player selections, move replacements, item receipts, original level provenance, service use, parent identity and alternative historical routes remain generally unrecorded on detached PK3/PK4 snapshots. A contradicted exact method never excludes all candidate routes and must **never** become an Invalid specimen verdict on its own.

**Stage 3** should strengthen the full *single-Pokémon/single-history* four-slot chronology and machine/level/evolution interleaving, with alternate histories maintained as Unknown. **Stage 4** covers complete Pal Park and Gen IV methods. **Stage 5** requires native PK1–PK4 fixtures and coordinator-only production integration recommendations. No merge is authorized.
