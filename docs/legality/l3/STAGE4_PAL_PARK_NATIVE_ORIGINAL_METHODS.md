## Substantial read-only engineering

- **`Gen14CandidateMovePalParkNativeJoint.h`**: replays one hypothetical Pokémon's independently attested Gen III history with Stage 3's complete four-slot verifier; checks original identity, exact departure and immediate NDS arrival snapshots; blocks an undeleted Gen III HM at the migration edge; replays source-specific native Gen IV level-up offers in order, machine teaching, tutor, reminder, independently verified Move Deleter shifts and Gen IV cartridge visits. Four physical move slots, legal range 1–467, level monotonicity, species identity and TM-receipt uniqueness are enforced. A new game visit alone does NOT trigger retroactive level-up offers. The status is **candidate-local**, never a whole-Pokémon Invalid decision.
- **`Gen4PlatinumNativeMethodData.h`**: from original [pret/pokeplatinum@fe1ea418a7364c93daf0d07ae55e195452d8803c](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/tree/fe1ea418a7364c93daf0d07ae55e195452d8803c) `res/pokemon/{bulbasaur,ivysaur,venusaur,charmander,charmeleon,charizard,squirtle,wartortle,blastoise,gastly,eevee,deoxys}/data.json`: **12 fully transcribed species records**, **174 ordered level-up offers**, **429 TM/HM compatibility entries**, and **100 tutor entries** (counts include repeated tutor entries as in the original source). Only Platinum is certified. Other species and Diamond/Pearl/HGSS native acquisition methods are unmodeled, hence `Unresolved`. Deoxys is normal-form only, with an explicit base-form witness required by the bridge. Native move IDs use original Generation III move constants and the stable Generation IV numeric move mapping.
- **`Gen4PlatinumPinnedMachines.h`**: 12 exact original Platinum `res/items/data/tmNN.json` / `hmNN.json` numbered machine-to-move identities. TM06 Toxic (92), TM10 Hidden Power (237), TM24 Thunderbolt (85), TM30 Shadow Ball (247), TM35 Flamethrower (53), TM53 Energy Ball (412), TM75 Swords Dance (14), TM90 Substitute (164), HM01 Cut (15), HM02 Fly (19), HM04 Strength (70), HM06 Rock Smash (249). Other machine IDs are **Unknown**, NOT incompatible, until similarly source verified.
- **`Gen14CandidateMovePalParkNativeRetail.h`**: binds the new generic bridge to the full original Gen III Stage 3 retail source adapter and bounded original Platinum source methods. Included through the existing L3-owned `Gen14CandidateMoveHistory.h` compile graph.
- Distinct candidate-local negative paths include skipped native offers, contradictory specific teaching methods, duplicate verified single-use TM receipt IDs, unchanged Gen III HM at departure, non-retained arrival slots, incorrect move replacements and incorrect claimed final slots. A level-20 transfer followed by a level-21 Platinum Bulbasaur offer cannot silently omit the original level-21 Sweet Scent. A deleted Gen III HM can be reacquired **after** Pal Park through an independently witnessed native HM teaching step.
- All source history packets are independent evidence supplied by callers. A detached PK3/PK4 does NOT include actual trainer choices, consumed TM receipts or transfer-stage snapshots. The renderer and legality engine are not modified.
