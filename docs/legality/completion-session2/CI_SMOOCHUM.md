## Source adjudication

The unhatched level5 Smoochum knows Pound1, Lick122, Meditate96 and Blizzard59. Pound/Lick are its initial moves; Meditate is an egg move and Blizzard is a TM/level move above its initial egg level. An egg cannot be taught the TM after creation. The original [Gold/Silver breeding script](https://github.com/pret/pokegold/blob/ef0201d8daf47e8b3ea1518eacf890f37d4cd5e8/engine/pokemon/breeding.asm) and [Crystal script](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/blob/3bc8daa4173e96a7f4011dad3922eb6fa5dad5c6/engine/pokemon/breeding.asm) select one donor in `GetHeritableMoves` and iterate that donor's current moves through `GetEggMove`. A compatible TM must therefore already be known by that donor; it cannot be supplied independently by the mother.

Jynx is female-only. Pairing her with Ditto selects **Ditto's** move list, as the gender-zero branches of `GetHeritableMoves` show; it does not copy her Blizzard. Ditto's native Transform does not establish a permanently stored Blizzard. The ordinary male HumanShape donors are species63,64,65,66,67,68,96,97,106,107,122,125,126,237. Reading the native base, evolution, level, egg and Gen I machine tables proves a direct no-entry invariant: neither a donor nor any of its pre-evolutions has a native/old-machine Blizzard source or accepts Blizzard through its egg list. Evolution and arbitrarily many breeding generations cannot introduce that absent move. This is more specific than treating an exhausted search as evidence.

The separate Python audit reconstructs those donors and all pre-evolutions from pret assembly, checks this invariant, checks Jynx's sex and excludes hatched records. It also inspects both original GB gift resources, every language, and disables adjudication if a guarded family, Smoochum/Jynx or Ditto has a catalogued Blizzard seed. The two reference resources were compared directly and are byte-identical. Catalog inspection is not authentication of every historical distribution. There is no newly authenticated event payload here; a future authenticated exception requires revisiting the rule, and even a new catalogued exception makes the gate fail for review. Glitch-created permanent move changes are outside an ordinary daycare acquisition proof and are not certified by this predicate.

The [newer PKHeX egg verifier](https://github.com/kwsch/PKHeX/blob/6042efa241a9925ce57e645329ea7f976deabdd3/PKHeX.Core/Legality/Verifiers/Egg/EggVerifier.cs) gathers EggMove and, for female-only species, InheritLevelUp methods. It does not gather inherited TM methods. Thus the chain check receives Meditate without the jointly inherited Blizzard constraint. This explains why the raw oracle result can be Valid while the original game's donor requirement fails. Production's stricter result is preserved; no production assertion or fixture expectation was weakened.
