# ENCOUNTER-004 / RNG-004 — source-attested Gen IV wild encounter and RNG join

**Purpose:** Existing exact-game wild slot tables and Method J/K PID/IV correlation have extensive independently pinned source coverage, but matching *some* slot and *some* PID seed is not an attested capture history. `Gen4WildSourceLinkedHistory` now binds an **exact original packed encounter row index** (including physical slot, location, range, form and game), the observed original PID + six 5-bit IVs, a derived Method-1 pre-PID seed, and a **separately attested exact historical lead family** to the same proposed original capture stage.

The original game, original caught species/form, met location/level, trainer-ID source for Honey Trees, PID/IV stage and Method-1 family are independently attested flags. The verifier **does not invent these facts from a traded/evolved/current PK4**. Only the supported normal Method J/K no-lead, Synchronize, and pinned no-lead/bug contest reroll branches are attempted for their *exact source row*. Unknown original lead or other PID mechanism remains Unknown; Cute Charm, Chain Shiny, Pokewalker, static/trade/event, extended lead histories and source-game transfers remain independently handled and cannot be globally rejected by a mismatch.

The stronger optional `evaluateNative` bridge binds a single **immutable checksum-valid encrypted PK4**, requiring independently attested unchanged species/IV/PID stage, exact source save group and same creature identity before comparing PID/IV/species/form to the proposed original. It intentionally declines evolved/traded candidates where those original stages were not independently evidenced. This is conditional historical consistency, not a statement that a present PK4 proves a wild source.

`evaluateUniverse` checks at most 8 **independently grounded** route identities against the SAME immutable original PK4 and historical observation. Duplicated/zero route IDs, missing root record, unverified alternatives, malformed/cross-game rows, incomplete Method-family evidence, or incomplete enumeration cannot yield a negative universal claim. Even `AllAttestedWildRoutesContradicted` is strictly limited to the externally claimed source-attested normal-Method-1 original-wild candidate universe. **It never emits global Invalid or disproves unrelated legal encounter methods.**

Native C++ regressions synthesize checksum-valid encrypted original Platinum and HeartGold snapshots using separately independently tested Method J slot-6 seed `469FB838` and Method K slot-4 seed `0000000D`, then validate exact row + original source identity, independently reconstructed Method-1 IVs, original six IV constraints, lead provenance, final unchanged source PK4 bytes, positive/negative/Unknown candidate universes, duplicated routes, external completeness and missing source flags. These synthetic original snapshots are test fixtures, **not external retail archival evidence**. No write paths, UI, Gen V, Master Vault or umbrella PR #103 integration.

### Wrong-row provenance is not negative evidence
A row index outside the source table or from a different game has no valid source-grounded history for the claimed original encounter. The single-route check returns `IncoherentCandidateRow`, and the alternative-universe aggregator marks it **unverified**, not contradicted, even if a caller sets its method-coverage flag. Regression tests distinguish a valid same-game encounter row with conflicting species/location (a genuine candidate-local contradiction) from a borrowed HGSS row against a proposed Platinum origin (missing/incorrect provenance, hence Unknown).

### Five-game, two-lead matrix
The test suite now also builds **ten independently separate synthetic source PK4/native witness cases**: Diamond, Pearl and Platinum Method J with both ordinary and Synchronize, and HeartGold and SoulSilver Method K with both ordinary and Synchronize. Each game obtains its own exact pinned original encounter row, synthesizes the original encrypted non-egg PK4 from a known four-draw PID/IV seed, verifies that reversing the six canonical IVs independently recovers the same Method-1 origin seed, and joins the exact historical slot and lead. This proves five-game API parity for the tested ordinary methods, not exhaustive coverage of all in-game encounter methods.
